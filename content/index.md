# 📁 Re:Fault Archive

_An archive of stories, experiments, and second attempts._

Hi, I'm Fauzi (AKZ). Welcome to my little corner of the internet.

Re:Fault Archive is where I collect the things I make, the ideas I explore, and the worlds I build along the way. From dark fantasy and dystopian fiction to games, visual experiments, and the occasional lighthearted project, this archive is home to all kinds of creative endeavors.

Some projects grow into entire fictional universes. Others begin as random thoughts, unfinished drafts, or experiments with tools I've never used before. Some might take years to develop; others might remain as little fragments of an idea.

Here, you'll find stories and characters, world-building notes, project documentation, development logs, and whatever else I decide to put together. Not everything is finished, and not everything needs to be.

This isn't a professional studio or a catalogue of perfect creations. It's a personal archive of things I'm passionate about making, learning, and sharing.

**Every mistake leaves something behind. Every unfinished idea has somewhere to go. And every new attempt is another chance to make something worth keeping.**

Take a look around. You might find a story, an experiment, or something that shouldn't have worked but somehow did.

Welcome to Re:Fault Archive.

— Fauzi (AKZ)
